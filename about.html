About

About Laurel Way Partners

Laurel Way Partners acquires established DWI and criminal-defense law practices in the New York City area from owners ready to step back. It is led by a New York attorney with a background in large-firm M&A and tax.

Laurel Way Partners is an attorney-led firm that buys and runs established DWI, criminal-defense and other flat-fee practices in New York City, Long Island and Westchester. Practices stay lawyer-owned, staff and client relationships carry over, and every conversation is confidential. Most of the price is paid at closing.

Who runs Laurel Way Partners

Laurel Way Partners is led by a New York attorney whose background is large-firm mergers and acquisitions and tax: structuring, documenting and closing acquisitions, and understanding how they are taxed. That is the work a practice sale needs, done from the buyer's side of the table by someone who knows what a seller's lawyer and accountant will look for.

You'll know exactly who we are from the first call. When a sale goes ahead, the written notice your clients receive under New York Rule 1.17 identifies the buyer, including bar admissions and years in practice in New York.

What we do

We buy established practices with $500K to $5M in annual fees, mostly billed at a flat fee or advance retainer. DWI and DUI defense comes first. We also look at misdemeanor criminal defense, traffic and license matters, wills and trusts, uncontested divorce, residential real estate closings and Chapter 7 consumer bankruptcy. We buy practices to own and run them for the long term. In each purchase, the buyer is a lawyer or a law firm owned entirely by lawyers, and the client notice Rule 1.17 requires names that buyer.

What we believe

  • A law practice belongs in a lawyer's hands. Every practice we acquire stays 100% owned by lawyers.
  • Clients come first at every step. They are told about the sale in writing, keep the right to choose other counsel, and keep their fee agreements.
  • Confidential means confidential. Nobody at your firm, in your courts or among your referral sources hears about a conversation with us unless you decide they should.
  • Straight answers, early. If a practice isn't a fit, we say so on the first call or as soon as we've seen the summary numbers, not after weeks of diligence.
  • Your people are the practice. The staff who know your clients and your courts are a large part of what we buy, and we plan to keep them.

What we are not

We are not a broker, a fund or a lender, and this site does not offer legal services to the public. Laurel Way Partners is not affiliated with any other firm using the Laurel name.
