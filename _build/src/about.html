[ENTITY NAME] is an attorney-led platform that partners with elder law, estate planning, and probate and trust administration firms in the New York metro area and northern New Jersey.

What we do

Owners get two things: liquidity, and a services team that handles marketing, intake, billing and administration, technology and hiring support. That leaves lawyers free to practice.

Owners who want to keep practicing can sell a minority stake and stay in charge of their firms. Owners who are ready to retire can sell their practice on their own timeline, with their staff kept on and their clients looked after. Partnering and retiring each have their own page.

Why elder law, estate planning and probate

An estate plan is written long before it's used. A will signed today may not reach probate for twenty or thirty years, and a power of attorney or health care proxy matters most when a client can no longer speak for themselves.

That makes continuity the heart of this work. Clients and their families need a lawyer who will still be there, original documents need careful safekeeping, and many firms are run by one owner who has built the practice over decades. Planning for what comes next protects the clients as much as the owner.

Why New York and northern New Jersey

This is a region of long-standing community practices, built on relationships with families, local courts and the financial advisors, CPAs and care managers who refer clients. Staying within Long Island, Westchester, the five boroughs and northern New Jersey means working with firms and a court system close to home.

Attorney-led

[ENTITY NAME] is attorney-led. Every law practice it works with stays owned by lawyers, and legal decisions stay with the lawyers who make them. [ENTITY NAME] itself is not a law firm and does not provide legal services.
