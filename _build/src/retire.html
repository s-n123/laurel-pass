A fair purchase, done by the rules

The purchase follows Rule 1.17, New York's rule on selling a law practice. It sets what clients must be told and protects their right to choose another lawyer.

The price reflects your practice and is set after a review under an NDA. It's covered by a mix of bank financing and seller participation, such as part of the price paid over time. Every term is in writing before you commit.

You pay no fee to talk, to share information under an NDA, or to close. Your own lawyer and accountant should review the terms.

Your clients and their original documents

Estate planning clients trust you with documents that may not be used for decades. Original wills and other documents are handed over to the buying attorney with a written record. Each client is told about the sale in writing, as the rules require, and keeps the right to choose another lawyer and take their file.

Your staff

The people who know your clients and your files are a large part of what makes the practice work. Keeping them on is part of the arrangement.

A structured handoff into retirement

The handoff is planned around your timeline. It includes introductions to clients, to their families where appropriate, and to the advisors, CPAs and care managers who send you work.

The rules on selling a law practice generally require you to stop practicing privately in the area. Any role you keep during the transition is agreed in writing, with your own lawyer, before closing.

What stays the same

  • Your name on the door, where the professional rules allow.
  • Your clients, cared for by lawyers who will still be there when their plans are used.
  • Your staff, kept on.

How a conversation works

  1. A confidential call. A first conversation about your practice and when you'd like to step back. Nothing to prepare.
  2. An NDA. A nondisclosure agreement is signed before any detailed information about your practice changes hands. Client confidences are shared only as the professional rules allow.
  3. Fit. A look at the practice together, and a plain answer on whether it's a match.
  4. Structure. The price, the financing, the handoff and the client notice, all in writing.
  5. Close. Documents are signed, clients are notified as the rules require, and the handoff begins.
