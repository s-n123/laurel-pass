What stays the same

Your name on the door.

The firm keeps the name your clients and referral sources know, as the professional rules allow.

II.

Your clients.

The families you've planned for can stay with the firm they know, and their files and original documents are kept with care. Choosing a lawyer is always their right.

III.

Your professional judgment.

Legal decisions stay with lawyers. The services team handles the business side and never directs legal work.

IV.

Your staff.

The people who know your clients and your files are part of what you've built, and the plan is to keep them on.

The services team

What we take off your desk

The business of running a firm, handled for you, so you can spend your time with clients.

  • Marketing

    Your website, search listings, reviews and the materials you share with referral sources. You approve anything that speaks for the firm.

  • Intake

    Answering new inquiries and scheduling consultations, under your firm's direction. You decide who becomes a client, and only lawyers give legal advice.

  • Billing and administration

    Invoicing, collections, bookkeeping support and the day-to-day running of the office. Trust accounts and fee decisions stay with the lawyers.

  • Technology

    Practice software, digital file storage, phones and security, set up and kept running.

  • Hiring support

    Recruiting paralegals, staff and associates when the practice needs them. You decide who joins.

How a conversation works

From first call to close, six to twelve months is typical.

  1. 1

    A confidential call

    A first conversation about your practice and what you want next. Nothing to prepare.

  2. 2

    An NDA

    A nondisclosure agreement is signed before any detailed information about your practice changes hands. Client confidences are shared only as the professional rules allow.

  3. 3

    Fit

    A look at the practice together, and a plain answer on whether it's a match and which path makes sense.

  4. 4

    Structure

    Terms in writing, including how you're paid: usually bank financing plus seller participation, such as part of the price paid over time.

  5. 5

    Close

    Documents are signed, and the transition begins on the schedule you agreed.
