The services team

What we take off your desk

The business of running a firm, handled by people who do it every day, so you can spend your time with clients.

  • Marketing

    Your website, search listings, reviews and the materials you share with referral sources.

  • Intake

    Answering new inquiries and scheduling consultations. You decide who becomes a client.

  • Billing and administration

    Invoicing, collections, bookkeeping support and the day-to-day running of the office. Trust accounts stay under the lawyers' control.

  • Technology

    Practice software, document storage, phones and security, set up and kept running.

  • Hiring support

    Finding and training paralegals, staff and associates when the practice needs them.

How a conversation works

From first call to close, six to twelve months is typical.

  1. 1

    A confidential call

    A first conversation about your practice and what you want next. Nothing to prepare.

  2. 2

    An NDA

    A nondisclosure agreement is signed before any details change hands.

  3. 3

    Fit

    A look at the practice together, and a plain answer on whether it fits and which path makes sense.

  4. 4

    Structure

    Terms in writing, including how the deal is financed: usually a mix of bank financing and seller participation.

  5. 5

    Close

    Documents are signed, and the transition begins on the schedule you agreed.
