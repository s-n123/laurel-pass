For brokers, CPAs and succession advisors

Laurel Way Partners buys established DWI, criminal-defense and other flat-fee law practices in New York City, Long Island and Westchester with $500K to $5M in annual fees. If you advise an owner who is thinking about stepping back, here is what we buy, what we need for a first look, and how we treat your client.

What we need for a first look

Summary figures are enough to tell you whether a practice fits, and none of them identify clients:

  • Practice areas and the rough share of fees from each
  • Annual fee revenue for the last three years
  • Approximate matter volume per year
  • Where new matters come from
  • Number of staff and the owner's preferred timing

We'll sign a nondisclosure agreement before anything is shared if you or your client prefer.

How we treat your client

  • We work through you. If your client has engaged you to run the process, we deal with you and don't go around you.
  • A fast, honest answer. If the practice isn't a fit, you'll hear that after the first look, not after weeks of diligence.
  • Clear terms in writing. Our letter of intent states the price, how much is paid at closing, and the terms for any balance.
  • Well-advised sellers. We encourage every owner to have their own lawyer and accountant review the deal.
  • Confidentiality. Nobody at the practice, in its courts or among its referral sources hears about a conversation with us unless the owner decides they should.

Who we work with

Law practice brokers, accountants and financial advisors to lawyers, bar association succession and practice-continuity committees, and the personal representatives and families of lawyers who have died, become disabled or gone missing. New York Rule 1.17 allows a personal representative to sell a practice, and a prompt, orderly sale protects the clients and the value of what the lawyer built.
